Terms of use

Updated 23 August 2026

By using GoDream, you accept the following. It is short because there is not much to say: the app helps you move towards a dream, one small step a day.

What the app does

GoDream breaks a dream you write into micro-actions and offers you one a day. The breakdown is produced by a language model: it can be wrong, suggest a badly sized action, or misread your dream. You can mark any action as too hard to have it broken down further.

What the app is not

GoDream is not medical advice, psychological support, or professional, financial or legal advice. The actions offered are suggestions; you remain the only judge of what you undertake, and solely responsible for your decisions and your safety.

Your content

The dream you write and your why belong to you. You grant us only the right to process them in order to provide the service described above — breaking them down, displaying them, backing them up if you have an account. Nothing more.

Acceptable use

Do not use the app to prepare an illegal activity, nor to circumvent or overload our servers. The breakdown service costs money per call: clearly automated use may be blocked.

Subscription

The free version allows one active dream. The premium subscription lifts that cap and unlocks the features announced with it. Billing, renewal, cancellation and refunds are handled by the App Store or Google Play, under their terms and from your account settings with them — not from GoDream.

Availability

The service is provided "as is". The app works offline, but breaking down a new dream depends on a third-party service that can be unavailable; in that case the app offers a fallback plan rather than leaving you without an action for the day. We may change or discontinue features; a shutdown of the service would be announced in the app.

Termination

You can stop at any time: delete your account from Settings, or simply uninstall the app. We may suspend an account that does not respect the acceptable use above.
